How TravelFusion Works

Three steps to a fully automated, seamless journey — from your doorstep to your destination.

1
Enter Your Journey

Input your source, destination, travel date and budget. Our AI engine activates instantly.

2
AI Generates Routes

Get 4 optimised plans — Cheapest, Fastest, Recommended, and Premium — with full leg breakdowns.

3
Book & Track Live

Confirm your plan. Drivers are dispatched automatically. Track every leg in real time on the map.

Capabilities

Engineered for Autonomous Travel

Multi-Modal Routing

Combines first-mile cabs, long-distance trains/flights, and last-mile autos into one seamless itinerary with a single booking.

Live GPS Tracking

Real-time Leaflet.js map tracking with animated route markers, ETA updates, and active trip status per leg.

SOS Emergency System

One-tap SOS with GPS-pinned coordinates dispatched to emergency contacts and admin monitors with audio alerts.

Gemini AI Plans

When configured, Google Gemini generates hyper-personalised route plans with real operators and live fare estimates.

Dynamic Fare Engine

Cheapest, Fastest, Recommended and Premium pricing models adapt to real-time demand, distance and transit modes.

Driver Dispatch Network

Auto-assigns vetted driver partners for first/last mile legs. OTP-verified trip start, earnings credited on completion.
